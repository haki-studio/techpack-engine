# Pixel Sky fleece jacket — intake brief (2026-10-19)

First fleece for Haki. Source: founder mockup `inputs/front.png` (front only, AI render, 1500 px).

## What the mockup shows
- **Shell:** high-pile sherpa fleece. Upper body and sleeves are sky blue; the lower ~40 % of the body and sleeves is cream.
- **Pattern:** a pixel-art cloud/wave border where blue and cream meet, with checker "pixel" edges and small blue pixel waves scattered in the cream.
- **Motifs:** a black pixel bird silhouette on the wearer's left chest, and yellow/gold pixel sparkles across the chest and on the left sleeve.
- **Collar:** stand collar, about 9 cm. The outer face is smooth blue woven (taslan/nylon look) and the inside is cream sherpa. A navy binding runs round the collar seam.
- **Closures:** full-length black centre-front zip with a blue pull. Two vertical zip pockets in the lower front, with navy tape and blue pulls.
- **Edges:** navy bound hem and cuffs, gathered (elastic) at the cuffs.
- **Fit:** dropped shoulder, boxy, cropped at the hip.

## The key production question: how is the pattern made?
The pixel clouds cover about half the garment, so they are almost certainly **not embroidery**. Embroidering that much area on sherpa would be enormously heavy and stiff. The realistic options are:
1. **Engineered jacquard sherpa:** the pattern is knitted into the pile, placed per panel. Needs a mill that can do it, a pattern grid at the knit gauge, and usually an MOQ.
2. **Pieced colour-block:** blue and cream sherpa joined along the cloud line, with the pixel detail as appliqué or embroidery. Cheaper, but the pixels are simplified.
3. **Embroidery:** only for the bird and the sparkles, on top of either option. Use a water-soluble topping so the stitches sit on the pile.

**Founder decision 2026-10-19:**
- **Sparkles** are embroidered (yellow + gold).
- **Bird** goes into the knitted pattern with the clouds if the mill can add a third colour (black) in that area. Otherwise it is embroidered too. Founder: "if we have to embroider the bird too, then we'll go with that".
- **Clouds:** option 1 (jacquard) is preferred. Confirm with the factory.

Build both bird versions: a jacquard grid cell map and an embroidery file. The factory answer then decides between them without a rebuild.
